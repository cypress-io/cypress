Note on the darwin-arm64 ffmpeg build
-------------------------------------

@ffmpeg-installer/darwin-arm64@4.1.5 was compiled with the --enable-nonfree
configuration flag and its own version banner reports that it is "not legally
redistributable". That build, as published by its distributor, cannot be
redistributed under the GPL. It is listed here so the notice is accurate; it
should be replaced with a build that does not enable nonfree components before
Cypress relies on it for distribution.
